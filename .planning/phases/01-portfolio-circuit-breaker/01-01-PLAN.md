<objective>
Build the portfolio circuit breaker's core: compute portfolio-wide drawdown from settled `pnl_units`, persist a trip flag that survives process restarts, gate the automated buy path on it, and provide a human-only, audit-logged, accountable reset.

Purpose: SAFETY-01 and SAFETY-02. Today nothing stops automated real-money entry when the portfolio is cascading — the only existing breaker (`max_daily_loss_usd`) silently un-trips at local midnight and silently un-trips again the moment a later win pushes the day's P&L back above its threshold. That is precisely the "silent auto-resume" SAFETY-02 forbids.

Output: a new `portfolio/circuit_breaker.py`, a `RuntimePaths` property for its state file, a configured threshold (D-03), the gate inside `AutoPolymarketBuyer.evaluate_and_execute()` (D-01), and an accountable CLI reset (D-05). The existing daily breaker is left exactly as it is (D-02).
</objective>
