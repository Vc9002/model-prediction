<objective>
Remove the 1.0U sizing cap on moneylines (every sport, every paper tier) and on NCAAF total Overs, so both size by edge the same way spreads and totals already do. This follows the operator directive (Vincent, 2026-11-25): "remove the 1U cap so moneylines and NCAAF Overs scale like everything else" / "no units should be 1u flat, everything should be edge scaled".

Purpose: the ledger survey (rows created on or after 2026-11-18) shows every moneyline row at exactly 1.00U whatever its edge. Examples are NCAAB v7m (53 of 53 rows with edge above 3%), NCAAF cfb-structural v5/v6.2/v7, NBA nba-elo-trend-lr-v5, and NFL v4 and moneyline-margin-v1, on Main as well as Flat. NCAAF Overs are also pinned at 1.00U. That sizing is flat, not edge-scaled.
