# Reject one-sided placeholder rungs as the main line; exclude MLB single-inning totals

## Theory
NBA/WNBA/NFL main-line selectors rank rungs by |long ask - 0.5| only. A placeholder rung
quoted with no real bid (2c bid / 49c ask) wins that ranking, and the short side is then
priced at 1 - bid = 98c. MLB full-game selection excludes F5 slugs but not single-inning
totals (`-i4-0pt5`), which are typed `total` and trade near 50%.

## Evidence
- BKN@MEM 2026-11-14 05:35Z: 227.5 at 35c/47c (real) lost to 229.5 at 2c/49c; ledger under at 98c.
- UTA@LAL 2026-11-18: whole ladder 1c/51c; under at 99c.
- PHI@DET 2026-11-04: only +19.5..+29.5 alternates captured; +19.5 priced at 94c.
- Ledger sweep (all NBA/NFL/WNBA spread/total rows with a slug): NBA 26 of ~2,400 rows on a
  placeholder (21 with no two-sided rung that run, 23 superseded, 3 settled, ~+0.01u);
  NFL 2 (pre 11-12 band); WNBA 16 (season over).
- MLB: 1,252 `-iN-` total snapshots 09-29..10-28, mid ~0.475; no ledger pick below line 5.5, so latent.

## Change
- `cli/forecast_market_select.py`: `_is_two_sided_rung` (both asks in the NFL band 0.20-0.80),
  applied in the WNBA spread/total and NBA spread/total selectors (NBA: after latest-per-slug).
- `cli/forecast_nfl_support.py`: band applied to the short ask too.
- `data_sources/mlb_market_odds.py`: `_is_full_game_market` rejects `-i<N>-` slugs.

## Test
`tests/test_main_line_placeholder_rungs.py` from the real ladders; revert-and-fail proof.
Existing `tests/test_cli.py` NBA selector fixtures given two-sided asks (they had long only).

## Failure mode
Early-morning runs on games whose ladder is all placeholders now decline (unmatched) instead
of pricing a placeholder; later runs pick the game up once a real market exists.

## Rollback
Revert the three src hunks.
