## Real bug found: placeholder rungs selected as the main line
NBA/WNBA/NFL selectors ranked rungs by the long ask alone. A 2c bid / 49c ask rung beat a real
market and the other side was priced at 98-99c (BKN@MEM 11-14, UTA@LAL 11-18, PHI@DET 11-04).
Size: NBA 26 of ~2,400 checkable spread/total rows, 23 superseded, 3 settled (~+0.01u); NFL 2
(before the 11-12 band); WNBA 16 (out of season).
