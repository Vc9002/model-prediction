## Design

A shrink is `p' = sigmoid(s * logit(p))` with intercept 0 (no direction is introduced), `0 <= s <= 1`, applied to the
served probability of every contract of the shrunk market, stored as an artifact field so the frozen v5/v7 artifacts
stay byte-identical and their models price exactly as before (field absent => s = 1).

- s is fitted on the train window only, checked on validation, and reported once on the locked holdout
  (`validation.chronological_split`, 60/20/20 on complete dates). Disclose that holdouts were read before.
- Edge, sizing, gates and replay snapshots must all see the shrunk probability; exact-replay parity must hold.
- New ids are registered (production.yaml schema v3 + model.yaml mappings) as candidates; promotion, the
  auto-buyer whitelist (code constant + live auto_buyer_state.json) and AutoLog follow-ups are done by the
  orchestrating session after review.
