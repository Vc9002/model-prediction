# Legacy-tier (research/gated_research) staleness audit

**Note:** this SUMMARY was written by the orchestrator, not the executor subagent — the
subagent was killed by a session interruption after it had already made all three commits
but before it returned its final report. All code/doc work below is verified against the
actual commits and a live test run, not reconstructed from the subagent's intent.

## What was found

Read-only snapshot of `ledgers.db`: `research` and `gated_research` tiers both have **0 open
rows** (645/635 settled, last settled 2026-10-24). `scripts/merge_research_ledgers.py --dry-run`
confirms 0 rows left to copy into Main/Flat. Data-side, Phase 2 retirement is already safe.

Two live readers were found treating the frozen tiers as current evidence, both fixed:

1. **Calibration pipeline** (`src/model_prediction/calibration_pipeline.py`) ingested the
   frozen `research` tier as its own calibration cohort. The frozen 62-row
   `dota2-tiered-elo-v6` research cohort (SEVERE drift) was inflating
   `calibration_health.json`'s `severe_drift_models` even though the live Flat (n=281) and
   Main (n=253) cohorts were both MODERATE. Fixed: extraction now reads Main + Flat only.
   Commit `ae3c0ba8`.
2. **Polymarket edge scanner** (`src/model_prediction/portfolio/polymarket_scanner.py`)
   included frozen research rows in its logged-picks index; its cross-date match fallback
   could let a frozen row supply `p_model` for a live market. Every settled research contract
   already exists in Flat, so this also double-counted rows in the pooled Platt fit. Fixed:
   index now built from Main + Flat only. Commit `45d70c1b`.

Readers confirmed fine, left unchanged: daily/`settle` CLI (only settles open rows — none
exist), auto-buyer paper-mode read (can't produce a buy — gated/no open rows), dashboard
production-evidence card (labelled historical section, read-only), dashboard change
detector/cache, ledger parity, `audit_best_picks.py`, `audit_ledger_pnl.py`,
`model_performance.py`.
