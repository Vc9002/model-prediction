# quick-261121-nw1: wire nba-anchor-v10 into the daily NBA slates, Flat only

Source: `docs/NBA_REVIEW_AND_V10_PLAN_2026-11-20.md` sections 6, 8, 9;
`outputs/nba/v10_harness/ANCHOR_WIRING.md`, `G1.md`, `V91_ABLATION.md`.

## Binding decisions

- v9 keeps serving unchanged (edge-based sizing). v10 rows are written AFTER v9 has been priced and
  logged, through their own writer (`flat_ledger.append_evaluated`, not `_append_secondary_ledger`), so
  v9 rows, their order and their sizes cannot change. Any v10 failure is caught (broad `except`),
  logged, and reported in the slate result; it never raises into the v9 path.
- Flat only. No Main row (artifact `main_min_edge` is null; G2 not passed). Not added to any auto-buyer
  whitelist or `auto_buyer_state.json`; not registered in `config/production.yaml`.
- G1 failed, so the artifact ships lambda = 0: the v10 price is the anchor's, and the only possible edge
  is anchor vs the Polymarket ask (track T-B).
- Moneyline: skipped. `nba_anchor_v10.MARKETS` is spread and total only (`price_market` raises on any
  other market); a moneyline anchor would be new model work, not wiring.

## Inputs per game and market

- Model mean: spread = v9's served home-margin mean (`inference_inputs["mean"]`); total = the v9.1
  totals engine (`config/models/research/nba-total-margin-v9-1.json`, hash verified) on the same
  history and upcoming games as v9, with `season_types` from the ESPN scoreboard `season.slug`, so the
  B7 playoff offset works. Computed inside the slate after v9's priced contracts are built, fail-soft.
- Contract: the same Polymarket row v9 priced (most balanced rung). Spread line in the home-margin
  convention (`spread_away_line`; away covers iff home margin < line), asks away = long, home = short.
  Total: over = long, under = short.
- Anchors (`FallbackAnchorSource([consensus, polymarket_mid])`, both `CapturedAnchorSource`):
  1. Devigged sportsbook consensus from `odds/nba/<ET date>/espn_odds.jsonl` (the pre-tip capture;
     DraftKings only today). ESPN's `home_line` is negative when home is favoured, so the home-margin
     line is `-home_line`.
  2. Polymarket mid of the priced contract (mean of the low-side mid and 1 - high-side mid).
- A quote is a line plus a fair probability, but `AnchorQuote` carries one number. Both sources are
  converted to the anchor's implied mean in line space with the artifact sigma:
  `anchor_mean = line - sigma * Phi^-1(p_low)` (p_low = fair P(away covers) / P(under)). With
  lambda = 0 the anchored probability at the contract line is then `Phi((C - anchor_mean) / sigma)`:
  exactly the fair probability when C equals the quoted line, and the sigma-normal translation when the
  contract sits on another rung. The raw line, fair probability and books are stored on the row.
- Freshness: an anchor is used only if `decision_time - 90 min <= observed_at_utc <= decision_time`.
  90 min: the consensus capture worker writes every 10 min near tip and the Polymarket quote v9 prices
  is minutes old, while daily forecast runs are hours apart, so the limit admits this run's captures
  and rejects a previous run's line. `decision_time = utc_now()` is taken AFTER both anchor sets are
  loaded (CLAUDE.md timestamp-ordering rule).
- No anchor at all: Flat's `append_evaluated` forces every row to CALL / FLAT_CALL, so it has no
  reason-coded no-call rows. Such games are skipped and counted (`NO_CALL_ANCHOR_MISSING`). This
  overrides ANCHOR_WIRING item 6 (which logged `NO_ANCHOR_MODEL_ONLY` calls). No executable ask on the
  called side: skipped and counted (`NO_EXECUTABLE_ASK`), as v9 does.

## T-B flag (pinned, section 9)

Consensus anchors only (never the mid fallback), gross of fee, evaluated on both sides at the contract
line: `gap(side) = P_consensus(side at C) - ask(side)`; flag when the larger gap >= 0.030. The
consensus probability at C uses the sigma translation above. Stored with side, gaps and threshold.

## Row payload (`model_input_snapshot_json`)

anchor source, raw line, fair low-side probability, implied anchor mean, observed_at_utc, age, books;
model mean and its source (plus the v9 total mean for reference); lambda, sigma, blended mean;
contract line, side, probability, ask, fee, net edge, gross gap; T-B block; reason codes; decision time.
