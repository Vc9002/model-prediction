## What each fix changed

1. **Neutral-site intercept: the pooled-intercept lean is removed and a neutral term is re-estimated. Out of sample
   the term is not distinguishable from zero, so do not read this as a clean fix.**
   - `scripts/cfb_v71_fit.py::fit_stage2` fits `a_home*(1-n) + a_neutral*n + b*raw` on train, and
     `CFBStructuralV7Model.means` applies it via the new `MatchupFeatures.neutral`.
   - Train fit (n 5578, 350 neutral): home -2.02 (se 0.26), neutral +1.42 (se 0.88), neutral minus home +3.44
     (se 0.92).
   - Identical teams at a neutral site now lean toward ESPN's HOME label (margin +1.42, home win 52.7%) about as
     far as v7 leaned away (-1.62, 47.6%).
   - The neutral residual's sign flipped in both windows. Its magnitude got worse on validation (+1.34 to -1.79)
     and better on holdout (+2.02 to -0.97). All four are within about 1.5 se of zero.
   - Live smoke: a neutral copy of 401860919 moves from +7.99 to +10.37 (moneyline 69.0% to 74.1%).
2. **FBS-only CFBD covariates.** Attribution comes from the disclosed pre-pin exposure, so treat it as
   indicative. v7's stage 2 on the v7.1 features scored holdout MAE 12.503 against v7's 12.467; the final v7.1 scored
   12.506. Nearly all of the small holdout MAE cost therefore comes with fix 2, not the neutral term. `core.standardise_covariates(rows, population=...)` z-scores over the season's FBS
   teams and gives FCS teams none, in every season. Research uses `season_classes`; serving uses the ESPN FBS list,
   via `model.covariates`. The CFBD gammas were re-estimated on the 2016-2021 finals and the 16 CFBD walks re-run.
   The live store's FBS lists agree with the research rule on every team that played in 2025 and 2026.
3. **Serving fit time and points-only rows.**
   - `fit_decision`: min(run clock, `decision_time_for(kickoff)`), one fit per distinct time. The observation cutoff
     stays the run clock. `expected_games` is re-filtered at the fit time.
   - Games need `event_start_utc` (passed by the challenger); a missing one gives NO_CALL_V7_NO_KICKOFF.
   - Points-only rows are added only for `terminal_without_pbp` events. Live: 26 to 13 points-only games.
   - In a test, a 23:50 ET run that fit at the run clock moved a fixture margin from 10.57 to 7.55.
4. **404 retry (bounded).** `cfb_pbp_live`: an `http_404` is terminal only when fetched >= kickoff + 24 h, in both
   `_fetch_done` and `terminal_without_pbp`, the same grace as `no_plays`. There were 0 `http_404` records in the
   live manifest. This change applies to v7 too: it is a data-source fix.
5. **Feature snapshot.** v7.1 results carry `inputs`: fit and run time, neutral flag, classes, games played, expected
   metric values per side, and CFBD z per team. These go into `feature_basis.model_inputs`, and contracts get
   `feature_capture: True`. `_forecast_cfb_sport` then writes a `feature-capture-v1` snapshot
   (`replay_verified: false`), so the ledger feature payload is `available` with a non-zero
   `observed_feature_count`. v5 and v6.2 rows are unchanged.

Routing: `college_football.build_cfb_slate` dispatches `cfb-structural-v7.1` to the v7-family pricer with
`model_id` (without that branch it would have priced nothing as `no_pricer`). `price_challenger_slate`,
`routed_markets`, the loader and the rationale label all take the model id.
