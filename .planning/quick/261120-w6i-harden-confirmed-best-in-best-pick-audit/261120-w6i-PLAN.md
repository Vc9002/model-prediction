<objective>
Harden `CONFIRMED_BEST` in `scripts/audit_best_picks.py` so it stops labelling lucky small buckets as best picks. Also fix five first-sight basis bugs the review of commit 2a48135f confirmed.

Purpose: in forward use, the label has been anti-predictive.
- Tracker picks labelled confirmed went 19W-27L, -14.07U.
- Picks confirmed from buckets with fewer than 10 graded games claimed a 76% win rate and hit 34% (12-23, -18.18U). Buckets with 10-29 graded games went 7-4.
- Closing prices on confirmed picks moved against the logged price 27 times and for it 12 times.
- Cause: MIN_GRADED=4 plus a 60% win-rate / +15% ROI hurdle picks the luckiest of hundreds of small buckets (winner's curse).

The new bar is n >= 30 verified decisions, a Wilson lower bound above the bucket's mean price, ROI >= 15%, and non-negative closing-line value at both bucket and model-family level. The orchestrator's live scan found that essentially nothing in season passes; only MLB over 2-4.9% (n=105, out of season) does. That is the intended honest outcome.
