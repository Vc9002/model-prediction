## Not made, and why

1. **Key-number push mass:** failed the preregistered validation rule (cover log loss worse).
2. **Main executable-quote requirement for the champion** (pricing #3): a Main-gate change for v5 is an operator decision. v5.1 itself prices captured books only.
3. **Integer-odds round trip** (pricing #7) and the **Flat sizing bump** (pricing #10): shared ledger and sizing code for every model, outside a challenger build.
4. **v5's own side rules, conference flag, EPA reads and 09:00Z history cutoff stay as they are.** Changing them would change the champion; v5.1 carries the fixes.
5. **Tier offsets stay on the static conference table.** The 2026 "Pac-12" is a rebuilt Group-of-5 league, so a name-keyed Power tier would give Boise State +2.0 instead of -2.5. Season-specific tiers need their own fit.
6. **Home-side under-prediction** (models.md #6) remains: +1.62 pts on the holdout (v5 +2.03). The neutral-site intercept candidate lost on validation.
7. **Weather and QB adjustments** are applied at serving as in v5 but were never in any fit: history carries no point-in-time weather or injuries.
